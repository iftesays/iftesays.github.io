FAQ

Questions before you kneel

Will they really stay up?

That's what they're designed for. The silicone lining grips your pants and the two straps hold the pad over the joint, not behind it. If they slide on you anyway, the guarantee covers it.

Do they cut off circulation?

The straps sit above and below the knee instead of across the back of it, and they're wide, so the pressure is spread out. Snug them enough to hold, not to squeeze.

What size do I need?

One size fits most adults. The straps adjust to fit over jeans, work pants, or bib overalls.

Will the cap scratch finished floors?

The hard cap is made for subfloor, concrete and tile. For finished hardwood, put a drop cloth down the same way you would with any hard-cap pad.
